# Toolomaro project — master specification
Goal: promote toolomaro.com internationally to relevant businesses (accounting, legal, recruitment, marketing, e-commerce, real estate, translation, logistics, hospitality, education, IT). Research at least 40 countries for advertising revenue potential; do not claim a fixed official AdSense country ranking. Research country-specific business discovery sources, permitted APIs, directories and listing structure, with evidence and terms. Use authorized contact datasets and opt-in business email records only; no access-control evasion or unsolicited bulk mailing.
Windows Python application: asynchronous concurrent country workers for all configured countries, concurrent permitted source tasks within global and per-source limits; isolate failures per item. On malformed data, missing email or item-specific error, skip item and continue other permitted tasks. Respect 403 access restrictions, 429 Retry-After, CAPTCHA and source terms; bounded retries with backoff for transient errors, persistent retry queue and checkpoint/resume on network loss or restart. SQLite for internal state and logs. Validate email syntax and DNS/MX where appropriate; do not claim mailbox deliverability from DNS alone. Deduplicate addresses. Excel output toolomaro_emails.xlsx: exactly one worksheet and exactly two columns Email and Country (English country names), no other metadata. Export atomically, retain records if Excel locked. No secrets or contact datasets in GitHub.
Before changes read all four handoff files. Update PREVIOUS_TASK.md, CURRENT_TASK.md and NEXT_TASK.md with actual evidence and commit/test status at every milestone. Never mark unrun tests or research as complete.
